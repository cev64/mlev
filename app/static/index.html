      Does the model actually work?

      Walk-forward validation: train on every season before season N, predict season N, roll forward. Nothing the model sees during training comes from the season it is being scored on, so these numbers are what it would really have done.

      Predict upcoming fixtures

      Trains on everything played so far, then predicts what has not been played yet. Every number is a probability or a distribution — never a single pick.
